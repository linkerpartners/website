Privacypolicy

We collect only what a consultation needs and delete it promptly once the purpose is served.

What we collect

When you request a call, we collect the following.

  • Required: name, email, message
  • Optional: company or brand, direction, services of interest

How we use it

We use your information only for the following.

  • Confirming your request and replying
  • Scheduling the call and sending a quote

How long we keep it

We keep it for one year after the consultation ends, then delete it. If a contract is signed, we keep it for the period set by law.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share personal data outside the company without consent, except where the law requires it. If we need a partner expert's help, we ask for your consent first.

Your rights and contact

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop its use. Please email us at the address below.
